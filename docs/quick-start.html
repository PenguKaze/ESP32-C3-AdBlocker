ESP32-C3 AdBlocker

Quick Start Guide — Block ads for your entire network in 2 minutes.

What's This?

A tiny device that blocks 140,000+ ad, tracker, and malware domains for every device on your WiFi — phones, laptops, smart TVs, tablets. No software to install on each device.

Setup

  1. 1 Power On
    Plug the board into any USB power adapter (phone charger, power bank, etc.)
  2. 2 Connect to Setup WiFi
    On your phone or laptop, connect to:
    C3AdBlock-Setup (open network, no password)
  3. 3 Open Setup Page
    Open Chrome or Edge browser and go to:
    http://192.168.4.1
  4. 4 Enter Home WiFi
    Select your home WiFi network and enter the password.
  5. 5 Done!
    The board will restart and connect to your home WiFi. All devices on your network are now protected.

Viewing Your Dashboard

  1. Find the board's IP address (check your router's device list)
  2. Open Chrome or Edge and go to: http://<IP_ADDRESS>
  3. Username: anything | Password: printed on serial console at boot
